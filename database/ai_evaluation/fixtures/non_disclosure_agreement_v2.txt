MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (v2)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is dated as of October 1, 2024 ("Effective Date"), by and between Apex Biotech Ltd., an English corporation having its office at 14 Science Park, Cambridge, CB4 0EY, UK ("Apex"), and Zenith Pharma LLC, a Delaware corporation having its office at 800 BioPark Way, Boston, MA 02115, USA ("Zenith").

1. PURPOSE
The parties wish to explore a potential joint clinical development agreement regarding monoclonal antibody formulation, mRNA encapsulation, and targeted oncology drug delivery systems ("Purpose").

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means all non-public scientific, commercial, clinical, genomic data, intellectual property filings, and financial information disclosed by one party ("Disclosing Party") to the other party ("Receiving Party"), whether orally, visually, or in writing, marked as confidential or reasonably understood to be proprietary.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Confidential Information does not include information that:
(a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach by Receiving Party;
(b) was already known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure;
(c) is independently developed without reference to Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
(d) is rightfully received from a third party without obligation of confidentiality.

4. OBLIGATIONS AND TERM
4.1 Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information with the highest standard of care, but not less than reasonable care.
4.2 Receiving Party shall disclose Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, and legal advisors who need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations.
4.3 The obligations of confidentiality shall endure for a period of five (5) years from the Effective Date.

5. NON-SOLICITATION OF KEY RESEARCH PERSONNEL
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of two (2) years thereafter, neither party shall directly or indirectly solicit, induce, or recruit any scientific researcher or clinical lead of the other party without prior written consent.

6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, USA. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Boston, Massachusetts.
