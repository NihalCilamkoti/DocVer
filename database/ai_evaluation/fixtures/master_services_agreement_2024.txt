MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (2024)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024 ("Effective Date"), by and between Acme Corporation, a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 100 Main Street, Suite 400, New York, NY 10001 ("Client"), and CloudTech Solutions Inc., a Delaware corporation having its principal place of business at 500 Tech Boulevard, San Francisco, CA 94107 ("Service Provider").

1. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES
1.1 CloudTech Solutions Inc. shall provide cloud migration, data engineering, and artificial intelligence deployment services as described in attached Statements of Work ("SOW").
1.2 Deliverables shall be delivered according to the milestone schedules set forth in each applicable SOW.
1.3 Client may inspect deliverables within ten (10) business days following receipt and provide written notice of acceptance or non-conformance.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 Client shall pay Service Provider the fees specified in each SOW.
2.2 All invoices are payable Net 30 days from the invoice date.
2.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.0% per month or the highest rate permitted by law.
2.4 Total contract value for Year 1 is capped at $450,000 USD unless amended in writing.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for an initial term of twelve (12) months.
3.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days prior written notice.
3.3 Either party may terminate immediately if the other party breaches a material provision and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA SECURITY
4.1 Each party agrees to protect the Confidential Information of the other party using at least reasonable care.
4.2 Service Provider shall maintain SOC 2 Type II compliance and implement TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest.
4.3 In the event of a security incident involving Client Data, Service Provider shall notify Client within forty-eight (48) hours.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
5.1 Client retains exclusive ownership of all Client Data and Client Pre-Existing IP.
5.2 Service Provider retains ownership of its proprietary platform, tools, and background algorithms.
5.3 Work Product specifically created for Client under an SOW shall be deemed work-made-for-hire owned by Client upon full payment.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
6.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, SPECIAL, INCIDENTAL, PUNITIVE, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES.
6.2 TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY OF EITHER PARTY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED $500,000 USD.
6.3 The limitations in this Section 6 shall not apply to breaches of confidentiality under Section 4 or indemnification obligations under Section 7.

7. INDEMNIFICATION
7.1 Service Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client against third-party intellectual property infringement claims.
7.2 Service Provider's total indemnification obligation under this Section 7 is capped at $250,000 USD.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.
8.2 Any dispute arising hereunder shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware under AAA Commercial Rules.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
